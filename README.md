# Nexora-Retail-Excel-Sales-Analysis
Excel-based business analysis to identify why sales growth did not translate into profit growth.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions Answered
- Why are sales increasing but profits declining?
- At what discount level does profitability turn negative?
- Which categories and regions are loss-making?
- Which sales channels and reps underperform?

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Restrict discounts beyond the optimal threshold
- Focus on high-margin categories over high-volume sales
- Re-evaluate loss-making regions and sales representatives
- Shift strategy from volume-driven to margin-driven growth
